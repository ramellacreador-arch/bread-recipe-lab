Rules snapshot

The app can support recipe-derived label data, but legal wording and eligibility must be verified against current South Carolina Department of Agriculture guidance before sale.

Supported by the app

  • Ingredients can be ordered by descending formula weight.
  • Major allergens can be detected from ingredient names and labels, with manual additions.
  • Net weight is tracked in grams and ounces and shown on the label preview.
  • Business name, product name, address or SCDA ID, contact, lot, and package dates are available as product information fields.
  • The current configured disclosure text is printed on labels and can be reviewed before printing.

Verify before relying on it

  • Verify the exact disclosure wording, capitalization, placement, and required contrast with current SCDA guidance.
  • Verify current cottage-food eligibility for the specific recipe, especially dairy, egg, starter, fillings, toppings, and other higher-risk additions.
  • Verify whether current federal or state net-quantity, address/ID, allergen, product-name, and local-ordinance requirements add details not modeled here.
  • Confirm that any voluntary health claim is permitted; the app does not validate claims.
